\begin{frame}{The benchmark separates accuracy, structure, and speed}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item The ANN/CaNN branch is the strongest component for supervised implied-volatility calibration and Greek estimation.
    \item Sobolev augmentation improves Greek diagnostics in both branches, but its interpretation depends on the information regime.
    \item The baseline PINN provides a fast PDE-trained pricing surface; its Greeks require additional structure.
    \item The benchmark separates aggregate accuracy, spatial error structure, hard-region behaviour, and evaluation speed.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
